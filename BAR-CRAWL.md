# Friday Boardwalk bar crawl — sample route

**Decision: nothing here is booked. This is a walkable sample route for Friday night that fits around the 9/11 fireworks at the Inlet — five stops, all downtown, all within about eight blocks, ending at the one bar open until 2:00 AM.**

Friday, September 11. The group rideshares downtown at **4:30 PM** and the OC Rock & Ride fireworks/drone tribute is at the **Inlet around sunset (~7:10 PM, still TBA)**. The route below starts near the Inlet, breaks for the show, then walks north up the Boardwalk. Hours and addresses verified **August 23, 2026** — recheck the week of September 7.

## The route

| Time | Stop | Where | Why it's on the list |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5:15 PM | **Cork Bar** | 3 Wicomico St. | Small downtown local bar a block off the Inlet. Good first stop while everyone is still arriving and nobody needs a big room |
| 6:00 PM | **Crawl Street Tavern** | 19 Wicomico St. | Same block, ~60-second walk. Bigger, live music, actual food — this is the light-food stop before the show |
| ~6:50 PM | **Walk to the Inlet** | South end of the Boardwalk | Fireworks / drone tribute. Get there early on BikeFest weekend; it is a 5-minute walk from Wicomico St. |
| 8:00 PM | **Purple Moose Saloon** | 108 S. Atlantic Ave (Boardwalk at Talbot St.) | Boardwalk rock 'n' roll bar, live band, no-frills. The loudest stop and the most fun for a group this size |
| 9:30 PM | **Shenanigan's Irish Pub** | 309 Atlantic Ave (4th St. & Boardwalk), 410-289-7181 | Open daily from 11:30 AM. Irish pub right on the Boardwalk; easier to hear each other and easier to eat again |
| 11:00 PM | **Pickles Pub** | 706 Philadelphia Ave (8th St.), 410-289-4891 | Open daily 11:30 AM – 2:00 AM. The last stop by design — everything else may close before the group is done |

Total walk end to end is roughly **eight blocks**, all flat, all Boardwalk or one street behind it.

## Rules for a 12-person group

- **No reservations.** These are walk-in Boardwalk bars; they will not hold a table for 12 on BikeFest weekend. Expect to split into two or three groups at every stop and re-merge on the walk.
- **One named leader per stop** decides when the group moves. Nobody waits for a headcount at a bar door.
- **The meeting point is the next bar**, not the current one. If someone gets separated, they walk to the next address on the list. Everyone screenshots this table before leaving the house.
- **Pay as you go at each stop.** No 12-person tab; it will not close cleanly and it will cost the group an hour.
- **Grouped rideshares back to Berlin.** Nobody drives. The house is ~20–25 minutes away and surge pricing on BikeFest weekend is real — order early and fill every seat.

## Adjustments

- **If the Friday casino fallback happens instead**, run only the first two stops plus the fireworks, then rideshare to Ocean Downs. Do not try to do both the full crawl and the casino.
- **If it rains**, Crawl Street Tavern, Shenanigan's, and Pickles are all fully indoors; skip the Inlet walk.
- **Justin sets the pace.** Saturday has a boat, a dinner reservation at 8:00 PM, and Seacrets. Friday should not be the night that ruins it.
- Casino Pirates props stay small and removable per [THEME.md](THEME.md). Some Boardwalk bars will not seat a costume group; a hat and a pin are fine, a full pirate kit is not.
