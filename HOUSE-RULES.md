# The Official Rules of Justin's Last Call

*A legally questionable-looking but actually very sensible bachelor-weekend constitution.*

## Rule 1: Do not get Justin evicted before he gets married

This is the prime directive. The Airbnb allows our registered guests but says **no events, parties, or large gatherings**. Nobody outside the confirmed group comes back to the house. No exceptions, no “he seems cool,” and no twenty-minute afterparty that becomes sunrise.

## Rule 2: Party energy stays inside; the yard is witness-protection mode

The patio, grill, fire pit, driveway, and yard are quiet zones—normal conversation, no shouting, no drinking games, and no music carrying outdoors. The property has exterior cameras that record motion-triggered **video and audio**, so assume the house has receipts.

Inside does not mean “anything goes.” It means music, pool, cards, jokes, and drinks at a level that cannot be heard outside. The Airbnb's no-party rule still applies everywhere.

## Rule 3: Doors and windows stay closed when music is on

Air conditioning is cheaper than eviction. Close the doors, close the windows, and keep speakers away from exterior walls.

## Rule 4: Twelve Friday, then eleven

Friday night peaks at 12; Joe leaves Saturday and nobody takes his place. Only the confirmed overnight group enters the rental. Meet new friends at the bars; say goodbye to them at the bars.

## Rule 5: Four cars means four cars

Every driveway space is assigned. No surprise fifth car, no lawn parking, and no blocking the road. Car keys go away before house drinking starts.

## Rule 6: Drink hard, but play the long game

Nobody is required to drink and nobody gets pressured. Eat actual food, drink water, and help a friend switch gears when needed. Getting too drunk to board the paid tiki boat is not legendary; it is donating your seat to the dock.

## Rule 7: The buddy rule is undefeated

Nobody leaves Seacrets, the Boardwalk, casino, or a bar alone. If your buddy disappears, find him before the group moves. Nobody drives after drinking—rideshare and Beach Bus are already part of the plan.

## Rule 8: Casino money is not trip money

Set a gambling limit before entering Ocean Downs. Lodging, food, boat, tip, and ride money never touches a slot machine. No ATMs for chasing losses, and when the group says cash out, cash out.

## Rule 9: Respect the house

No smoking, no broken furniture experiments, no indoor ballistics, no glitter, and no mystery stains left for Sunday. Use coasters, clean spills immediately, and report damage to Justin rather than hiding it.

## Rule 10: Whoever creates the mess joins the cleanup crew

This is not punishment; it is physics. Sunday cleanup starts at 8:00 AM, regardless of Saturday's achievements.

## Rule 11: Outdoor voices become library voices after 10:00 PM

If the host specifies an earlier or stricter quiet time, that rule wins. When in doubt, go inside and lower the volume.

## Rule 12: Make it memorable for the correct reasons

The goal is great stories, not police reports, hospital bracelets, lost phones, missed reservations, or Airbnb support tickets.

## Group acknowledgment

By joining the trip, each guest agrees to protect the house, the bookings, the group, and Justin's security deposit. Violators owe the group breakfast and become first pick for cleanup duty.
