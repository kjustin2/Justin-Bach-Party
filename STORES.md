# Grocery and alcohol stores near the house

**Decision: one shared grocery run to the Ocean Pines Food Lion or the Berlin Walmart, and a separate stop at Berlin Beer/Wine & Liquor for anything alcoholic — Maryland grocery stores and Walmart cannot sell beer, wine, or liquor.**

Everything below is a short drive from **8628 Saddlecreek Drive, Berlin, MD 21811**. Distances are approximate planning guidance, not guaranteed drive times — check GPS the day of. Hours were verified **August 23, 2026** and should be rechecked the week of September 7.

## The one rule that trips people up

Maryland does **not** allow beer, wine, or liquor in ordinary grocery stores. Food Lion, Walmart, and the gas stations here sell **zero alcohol**. Every drop has to come from a licensed package store, and those close earlier than the grocery stores do. Plan the liquor stop first, the food stop second.

**Thursday timing matters.** Check-in is 4:00 PM and the Berlin liquor store closes at 7:00 PM Thursday. Hit it on the way in — the [itinerary](ITINERARY.md) already has a 3:15–3:45 PM grocery/ice stop, so combine the two while cars are still packed and nobody has been drinking.

## Suggested Thursday run

1. **3:15 PM — Berlin Beer/Wine & Liquor** (10818 Ocean Gateway). Whatever alcohol did not travel with a car.
2. **3:35 PM — Food Lion Ocean Pines** (11007 Manklin Creek Rd.). Grill dinner, breakfast, mixers, limes, ice last so it does not melt.
3. **4:00 PM — house.** Ice into coolers before anything else gets unpacked.

Send **one or two cars**, not four. Assignments by car are in [HOUSE-SUPPLIES.md](HOUSE-SUPPLIES.md#ownership-by-car).

## Late-night and gaps

- After liquor stores close, the only alcohol available is at bars and restaurants. Buy enough on Thursday.
- Ice runs Friday and Saturday: Food Lion Ocean Pines or Walmart, both open to 11:00 PM.
- The Saturday **Tipsy Tikis** cruise already includes beer, wine, spirits, and soft drinks. Do not buy for the boat, and do not bring glass.
- Nobody drives to a store after drinking. If the house needs something at midnight, it waits until morning.
