# Casino Pirates theme

The theme is **Casino Pirates**. We are **not ordering group shirts**. The goal is a small prop kit people can wear comfortably at the casino, restaurants, bars, the boat, and Seacrets without blocking views or bothering staff or other guests.

| Nicer captain bandana or small hat for Justin | 1 | TBD | Not bought |

Buy simple items that can come off quickly and fit in a pocket. Bring 15 sets so the 13 attendees have choices plus a couple of spares.

## Leave these home

- Plastic swords, replica weapons, hooks, or anything security could reasonably reject
- Masks or face coverings
- Oversized hats, flags, inflatable props, or anything that blocks aisles or views
- Glitter, loose confetti, noisy props, or items that could damage the Airbnb
- Matching shirts; the archived `shirts/` folder is not part of the current plan

Venue staff have the final say. If asked to remove a prop, put it in the pouch and keep moving.
