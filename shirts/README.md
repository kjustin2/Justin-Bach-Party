# Justin's Official Drinking Team Shirt Variants

`Official Drinking Team` is the only retained shirt direction. Every variant uses a small role-specific front and a larger bachelor-weekend design on the back. Justin receives a contrasting shirt color so he stands out in photos.

## 01 - Classic Drinking Team

- Justin front: `BUY ME A SHOT`
- Crew front: `I'M WITH THE GROOM`
- Back: clinking mugs with `JUSTIN'S OFFICIAL DRINKING TEAM`
- Best direct evolution of the selected concept.

## 02 - Kramer Brewing Co.

- Justin front: `HEAD BREWER`
- Crew front: `TASTING CREW`
- Back: brewery-seal version of `OFFICIAL DRINKING TEAM`
- Most wearable after the weekend.

## 03 - Varsity Drinking Team

- Justin front: `TEAM CAPTAIN`
- Crew front: `STARTING LINEUP`
- Back: athletic `26` roster design
- Cleanest and easiest to screen print.

## 04 - Official Tiki Drinking Team

- Justin front: `TIKI CAPTAIN`
- Crew front: `DECK CREW`
- Back: tiki glass and waves with `OFFICIAL DRINKING TEAM`
- Best fit for the Ocean City and Tipsy Tikis plans.

## 05 - Ocean City Bar Badge

- Justin front: `LAST CALL`
- Crew front: `BAR SUPPORT`
- Back: circular `OFFICIAL DRINKING TEAM / OCEAN CITY CHAPTER` badge
- Most understated option.

## Print files

- Each variant is in `drinking-team-variants/<variant>/artwork/`.
- Every variant includes separate Justin and crew front/back SVGs and transparent 300-DPI PNGs.
- Recommended front width: 3.5 to 4 inches.
- Recommended back width: 10.5 to 11.5 inches.
- Use a garment-dyed heavyweight tee and no more than two ink colors.

Run `python shirts/build_drinking_team_variants.py` to rebuild the package. The earlier web research remains in [`RESEARCH.md`](RESEARCH.md).
