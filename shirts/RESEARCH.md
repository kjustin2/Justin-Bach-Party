# Bachelor Party Shirt Research

The previous left-chest-only concepts were too subtle. Current examples and print guidance point to a better balance: a small role mark on the front, plus a recognizable shared bachelor-weekend graphic on the back.

## Patterns that work

- Clearly distinguish the groom from the crew through shirt color, role wording, or both.
- Put the groom's name, bachelor-party context, destination, and year somewhere visible.
- Use a front/back layout: small role mark in front, larger shared trip design on the back.
- Make the main joke readable from several feet away.
- Use one main joke and one supporting element rather than a paragraph of text.
- Keep artwork to one or two print colors with strong contrast.
- Destination-trip and vintage souvenir styling looks more wearable than generic novelty clip art.
- Personal jokes and groom-specific interests work better than generic `Groom Squad` wording.
