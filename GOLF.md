# Friday golf plan

## Recommended format

Reserve consecutive tee times around **8:00–8:30 AM Friday, September 11**. A normal 18-hole round will take roughly 4–4.5 hours, allowing the golfers to meet the non-golfers for the 1:15 PM group lunch.

Do not book until the player count is firm. Tee times usually hold four golfers, so:

| Golfers | Tee times needed |
| ---: | ---: |
| 4 or fewer | 1 |
| 5–8 | 2 consecutive times |
| 9–12 | 3 consecutive times |

## Course choice

### Recommendation: Eagle's Landing

- 12367 Eagles Nest Road, Berlin, MD.
- Scenic course overlooking Sinepuxent Bay and Assateague.
- Explicitly supports group outings and multiple skill levels.
- Best combination of destination feel, group organization, and value.
- Current rates are dynamic; use the official tee-time page or call the pro shop.

[Official course](https://www.eagleslandinggolf.com/) · [Group outings](https://www.eagleslandinggolf.com/golf-outings)

### Premium alternative: Rum Pointe

- Pete and P.B. Dye Scottish-style links course.
- 17 of 18 holes have bay views.
- Stronger “special trip” course, likely at a higher price.
- Online reservations are available and golfers pay at the course.

[Official course](https://rumpointe.com/) · [Book a tee time](https://rumpointe.com/ocean-city-md-golf-reservations/book-a-tee-time/)

### Convenience alternative: Ocean Pines

- Public course in Berlin.
- Rates must currently be confirmed by phone.
- Rental sets are listed at $40 for 18 holes or $50 for premium Callaway sets.
- Useful if several golfers are flying/driving without clubs.

[Official rates and rental information](https://www.oceanpinesgolf.org/golf/course-rates)
